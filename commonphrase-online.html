How To Play

Surah or juz names appear one at a time. Find a phrase that occurs in all the shown places. Tap your screen to buzz and recite the phrase immediately; checking is self-marked. A correct target answer earns one point.

The clues become unique when only one eligible answer fits them all. More names may appear afterwards as extra help. The selected minimum is a hard minimum for both targets and answers. The maximum limits question targets only; checking an answer or objection includes longer common phrases within an ayah. Exact shorter fragments of a matching longer phrase count as one answer; phrases which merely overlap stay separate. Longer wording containing the highlighted answer is acceptable.

Two players: with objections enabled, the opponent may object during the answer timer and recite at least two different eligible phrases, each occurring in every shown place. Check and tick different answer groups you named (a shared shorter fragment cannot count twice): score one point per confirmed phrase, with at least two required. If the clues were already unique, the objector loses one point and the buzzer gains one.

Three or four players: with early-buzz elimination enabled, buzzing before uniqueness ends the round and makes you sit out the next round. It does not subtract a point.

With these options disabled, early guesses are allowed, but must match the target answer. A wrong answer eliminates you for the current round while others continue. First to the chosen score wins. Targets do not repeat within a game. When the pool runs out, the highest score wins, with tied leaders drawing. Play Again resets the pool.

When all clues are visible, you can still buzz or use the host’s reveal-answer button.
