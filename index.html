Hifdh Arena

A small suite of tools for testing and sharpening Qur'an memorisation. The apps here are all unique, not just another version of typical Qur'an apps.

Smallest Phrase Hifdh-Tester

Shows you only the smallest portion of the Qur'an needed to uniquely identify where it is, and you need to find the ayah(s).
