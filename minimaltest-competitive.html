Smallest Phrase — Competitive

A local, pass-the-phone buzzer game built on the same shortest-unique-phrase engine as the single-player Smallest Phrase Tester.

Sit around one device. A word or two appears, then more are added one at a time — tap anywhere in your zone the moment you know the ayah.

How To Play

A part of the Qur'an will be revealed on the screen, appearing one word at a time. This can be from anywhere in the Qur'an, not just the beginning of an ayah. Players should have their fingers ready to buzz in their zone. Once the phrase (portion of the Quran) shown becomes unique (only comes once in the Qur'an - this could even be just the first word), the first player to (mentally) find the beginning of the ayah should buzz and recite it. There is no time to buzz and then think - the answer must be given immediately after buzzing, and the ayah should be recited at least from its beginning up until the displayed phrase (concession can be given for very long ayaat). If the player buzzes when the displayed phrase is unique and recites the ayah, they win one point.

However, if a player buzzes whilst the shown phrase is not yet unique (it comes more than once in the Qur'an), then what happens next depends on whether there are 2 players or more:

2 players: The opponent may 'object' immediately by tapping their screen. They then have a few seconds to name at least 2 places where the shown phrase comes in the Qur'an. If they are successful, then they receive points equal to the number of ayaat they recited where the phrase appears. If the phrase shown was not unique, but the player who objected did not manage to recite at least 2 ayaat, then no one gets the point. If the objection was false (the phrase was indeed unique), then the one who objected loses, and the other wins a point.
3 or 4 players: The point is lost, and the player who buzzed early is eliminated from the next round.

Both of the above (objection and round elimination) can be turned off, in which case early buzzing has no penalty.

The first player to reach the set point threshold wins.
