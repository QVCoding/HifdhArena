Quran Occurrence Tester

Test your memorisation by identifying specific occurrences of repeated phrases. You will be shown a phrase from the Qur'an and asked questions about where it comes in the Qur'an.

The minimum and maximum number of times the phrase can come in the Qur'an. If this number is very low, it may be very easy to guess. If it is too high, the phrase you are shown can be very common in the Qur'an, making it hard to guess. The default values are 2-5 occurrences.
All questions will be shown from the selected portion only. For "Find the Nth occurrence" and "How many times" questions, consider only this portion (the Nth time the phrase occurs in this portion, how many times does it appear in this portion).
